# Hello Kitty & My Melody — The Sweetest Day 🍦🍩

A tiny 3D cartoon (about 2 minutes) made for a 7-year-old. Hello Kitty and My Melody play in the park, dance ballet,
and learn about sharing when Cinnamoroll tries to grab the ice cream and the donuts.

▶ **Watch:** https://nikolays.github.io/vika/

Real-time Three.js. The music, voices and sound effects were generated with ElevenLabs.
Fan-made, non-commercial. Hello Kitty, My Melody and Cinnamoroll are © Sanrio.
